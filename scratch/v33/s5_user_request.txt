Use:


```text

```

prefer the current child member if present:

```text

```

Otherwise choose the first requested member.

```text

```

## B. Explicit listening / seeing / reacting

If clause contains:

```text

nhìn

```

prefer an adult listener if present:

```text

father

```

Example:

```text

```

Important:
the other person may be implied OFF-FRAME.
Do not ask Schnell to draw them.

---

## C. Reflection / emotional everyday moment

For abstract/reflection beats with no whole-group anchor,
prefer a single adult already present in the original beat.

Example:

```text

```

Do not invent a new person.

# 6. ZERO-PERSON / OBJECT SIMPLIFICATION RULES

Use:

```text
SCHNELL_OBJECT
```

```text

```

use:

```text

```

No people.

---

## B. Simple-meal / ordinary-life detail

For narration about:

```text

đời sống thật
```

if the sentence does NOT explicitly require all people together,
use:

```text

```

No people.

---

## C. Phone-away detail

For:

```text
điện thoại được đặt sang một bên
điện thoại không nằm giữa bàn
```

prefer:

```text
phone face-down on a small side shelf away from the dining table
```

No hand/person required.

This is safer than generating fingers/hands.

---

## D. Empty-after-activity trace


No people.
